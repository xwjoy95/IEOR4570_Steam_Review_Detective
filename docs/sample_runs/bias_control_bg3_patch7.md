# Why did Baldur's Gate 3 get a wave of negative reviews after Patch 7, and what were players angry about?

Between September 1, 2024, and October 31, 2024, about 96.7% of sampled reviews for **Baldur's Gate 3** were positive, meaning only 3.3% of the reviews in this window were negative [R86][R87]. Despite this overwhelmingly positive overall reception, a minor wave of negative reviews followed the release of Patch 7 on September 5, 2024, which introduced official mod support, new evil ending cinematics, split-screen improvements, and Honour Mode updates [R80][R86][R87]. Players who wrote negative reviews were angry that the new official mod manager broke existing game saves, disabled third-party mods, and caused persistent multiplayer connection and synchronization failures [R92][R98][R118].

## Timeline

* **June 2024**: **Baldur's Gate 3** has an average of 58,746 concurrent players and a peak of 103,767 [R75].
* **July 2024**: The game averages 66,694 players, peaking at 115,180 [R76].
* **August 2024**: Player counts remain stable with 66,700 average players and a 119,687 peak [R77].
* **September 5, 2024**: Patch 7 is released on PC, adding official modding support, split-screen improvements, new evil endings, and new Legendary Actions for Honour Mode [R80].
* **September 2024**: Average player counts rise by 9.43% to 72,993, with a peak of 136,574 [R78].
* **September 19, 2024**: Larian releases Hotfix #26 on PC to resolve a game crash and fix a bug where enemies retained Honour Mode Legendary Actions on non-Honour-Mode saves [R81].
* **October 1, 2024**: Patch 7 rolls out to console and Mac alongside Hotfix #27, which addresses modding experience issues [R80][R82].
* **October 16, 2024**: Hotfix #28 is released to fix crashes, blockers, and Mod Manager enable-disable loops during multiplayer [R84].
* **October 2024**: Average players drop by 15.04% to 62,014, with a peak of 120,185 [R79].

## What players say

Out of 600 sampled reviews across English and Simplified Chinese, 580 were positive and 20 were negative [R86][R87]. This indicates that the massive majority of players praised the game and remained highly satisfied during this period [R86][R87]. The negative review share was slightly higher among English players at 4.3% compared to Simplified Chinese players at 2.3% [R86][R87].

However, the critical minority focused heavily on technical issues introduced by the patch [R92][R98][R118]. The primary complaint was that the official mod manager corrupted mod libraries, broke existing save files, and stopped third-party mods from working [R92][R98][R113]. For example, a player with 363.8 hours of playtime complained: "I will change this once they fix their mod manager.  As of right now it breaks the game" [R92]. Another player with 298.8 hours noted: "funny how larians attempt to support mods basically destroyed everyones saves and made mods stop working" [R98].

Additionally, players experienced a bug where the manager would auto-resubscribe and re-download deleted mods, compounded by login loops on the modding website [R97][R99]. Others criticized broken multiplayer lobbies, desyncs, and lack of helpful error messages [R93][R118][R126]. A player with 78.3 hours stated: "My negative review hinges entirely upon terribly buggy and poorly thought out multiplayer. Bought the game to play with friends, and it just constantly has problems playing multiplayer" [R93].

Other complaints included general performance degradation, crashes on high-end PCs, and a frustrating new mechanic in the evil endings where non-Dark Urge players who used tadpoles were forced into a mind flayer transformation unless they passed a difficult DC 25 Constitution save [R94][R123][R127].

## What the data can't answer

The player count data cannot show exact daily correlations with the release of Patch 7 beyond monthly aggregated averages.

Furthermore, standard limits apply: Steam reviews only reflect the opinions of players who write reviews, and this data does not include console player counts. The developers' motives are only known as stated in their official announcements [R80][R81]. Finally, SteamSpy owner estimates were not provided in the findings.
