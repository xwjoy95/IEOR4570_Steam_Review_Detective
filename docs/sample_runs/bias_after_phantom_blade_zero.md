# How Do Reviews of Phantom Blade Zero Compare Now with the Launch Period?

Reviews for **Phantom Blade Zero** remain overwhelmingly positive, with recent English reviews showing 3.7% negative and Simplified Chinese showing 3.0% negative between November 5 and November 8, 2026 [R7][R8], compared to 5.3% negative for English and 3.3% negative for Simplified Chinese during the launch window of October 29 to November 4, 2026 [R9][R10]. This slight positive trend followed the release of Patch 1.0302256 on November 4, 2026, which addressed critical technical bugs like shader compilation stutters and control mapping issues [R6]. While early complaints heavily criticized performance drops and unresponsive controls [R54][R68], recent criticisms have shifted to minor environmental collision issues [R48], while both periods saw praise for the game's responsive combat, beautiful art direction, and music [R11][R15][R21].

### Timeline

*   **October 28, 2026**: **Phantom Blade Zero** is released on Windows by S-GAME Studio and S-GAME Publishing for $59.99 [R3]. S-GAME publishes the developer announcement titled "Reason and Madness" reflecting on franchise history [R5].
*   **October 29 – November 4, 2026**: Launch review period [R9][R10]. In sampled reviews, English reviewers submit 284 positive and 16 negative reviews, while Simplified Chinese reviewers submit 290 positive and 10 negative reviews [R9][R10].
*   **November 4, 2026**: S-GAME releases patch version 1.0302256, adding sound options, remapping for keyboard and controller, easing boss grab attacks on Wayfarer and Pathbreaker difficulties, and optimization improvements for PC shaders [R6].
*   **November 5 – November 8, 2026**: Recent review period [R7][R8]. Sampled English reviews contain 289 positive and 11 negative reviews, while Simplified Chinese reviews contain 291 positive and 9 negative reviews [R7][R8].
*   **November 8, 2026**: The game records a current player count of 349043 on Steam [R4].

### What players say

During the recent period, 96.3% of sampled English reviews and 97.0% of Simplified Chinese reviews were positive, maintaining the strong positive momentum from the launch period [R7][R8][R9].

#### Praise
Positive reviewers continuously praise **Phantom Blade Zero** for its butter-smooth, highly responsive combat system, outstanding fluid animations, and satisfying weapon diversity [R11][R15][R17]. Players also express appreciation for the game's engaging wuxia atmospheric music, great art direction, and Chinese voice acting [R15][R17][R18]. At launch, some players specifically praised the design choice of non-respawning enemies, though this was divisive as others felt it left maps feeling empty [R29][R70].
*   "BEST ACTION GAME I'VE EXPERIENCED IN THE RECENT YEARS." (57.8 hours played) [R11]
*   "Every weapon feels so different and unique while all feeling extremely satisfying to try out with the exclusive animations for them all." (59.8 hours played) [R15]

#### Criticism
Negative feedback has evolved since launch. In the launch window, players complained about severe shader compilation stutters, delayed texture loading, frame drops, frequent crashes, and AMD GPU glitches [R54][R56][R62]. They also criticized key remapping bugs and unresponsive dodge inputs [R55][R68]. Following the November 4 patch [R6], recent complaints highlight other optimization issues on GTX 50s [R46] and collision bugs where players get stuck on small ledges or rocks [R48]. Others complain about invisible walls [R43] or dismiss the combat across both windows as repetitive button-mashing or heavily scripted [R42][R52][R53].
*   "I should not be getting stuck on little ledges, of walk ways, rocks, pieces of wood, etc." (25.3 hours played) [R48]
