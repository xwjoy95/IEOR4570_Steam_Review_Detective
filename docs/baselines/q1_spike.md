In response to the massive review-bombing in August, Arrowhead Game Studios’ creative director, Johan Pilestedt, and the development team issued a public apology and committed to a **60-day turnaround plan** to address player concerns. 

This resulted in two major patches in **September and October 2024** that completely reversed the negative sentiment:
* **The September 17 Patch:** This massive update fundamentally overhauled the game's armor-penetration, health, and damage systems. It buffed over 30 weapons and stratagems, restored the Flamethrower to its former glory, and made enemies feel less like "bullet sponges."
