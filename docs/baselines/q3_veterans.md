### 1. Veterans (100+ Hours)
Veterans generally focus on the game's competitive integrity, long-term balance, and technical performance. Their reviews during this period would likely focus on:
*   **The "Meta" and Weapon Balance:** Veterans closely analyze incremental changes to recoil, weapon damage, and utility items. Their reviews will praise or highly criticize specific balance patches (e.g., if a certain weapon becomes too dominant).
*   **Anti-Cheat and Matchmaking:** A primary pain point for long-term players is the prevalence of cheaters or perceived failures in the anti-cheat system. They also focus heavily on ranked matchmaking quality and queue times in their respective regions.
*   **Map Reworks and Features:** If PUBG introduces a new map or majorly overhauls an old one around this time, veterans will review it based on tactical viability, loot distribution, and competitive flow, rather than just visual appeal.
*   **The "Love-Hate" Paradox:** Experienced players often leave "Negative" reviews despite having hundreds or thousands of hours logged, typically citing frustration with developer decisions, monetization, or performance issues, while still acknowledging the core gameplay loop is unmatched.

### 2. Newcomers
Newcomers evaluate the game through the lens of accessibility, initial excitement, and the learning curve. Their reviews would likely focus on:
*   **The Learning Curve and Onboarding:** Newcomers often comment on how difficult the game is to pick up. Reviews will praise or criticize the tutorial system, AI training matches, and how punishing the gunplay feels compared to more arcade-like shooters (like *Apex Legends* or *Call of Duty*).
*   **The Bot Experience:** Early matches in PUBG rely heavily on bots to ease players in. Newcomers either appreciate this as a way to learn the mechanics or complain that the matches feel too easy and unrewarding until they transition to fully human lobbies.
*   **Value and Performance:** As a free-to-play game, newcomers will assess whether the game feels "pay-to-win" (it generally isn't, but heavy monetization of cosmetics can confuse new players) and how well the game runs on entry-level hardware.
*   **The Core Battle Royale Thrill:** New players are much more likely to write enthusiastic reviews about the tension of survival, the excitement of getting their first "Chicken Dinner," and the scale of the maps.
