Steam Review Detective

What it explores: why a Steam game's player sentiment changed. Researchers check reviews, patch notes and player counts in parallel, and a writer turns what they find into a cited report.

Who it's for: players deciding whether to buy or come back to a game, and developers studying how a competitor's update landed.

How to use it: name a game and ask what changed. If the question is too vague, it asks you back first.
